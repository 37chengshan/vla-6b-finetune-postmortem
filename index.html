WHAT BROKE.
WHAT IT COST.
WHAT GOT THROUGH.

Ten days, eight AMD GPUs, one 6B vision-language-action model, and a submission window that almost closed without us.

The training was the part that worked. Everything here is read from a log we still have: 1,500 steps, 549,787 frames, 43.2%, one attempt left.

STORAGE

A thousand steps of checkpoints lived on a disk that died with the instance. Twenty-six GPU hours, gone in one reclaim.

SILENT
DEATH

A 4 GB tmpfs filled by compiler JIT and eight ranks exited with no traceback at all. The fix was one directory.

RESUME

A checkpoint that restarts from scratch logs the same line as one that resumes. Thirty-nine steps went to that.

GATES

A four-or-eight-GPU guard and a LoRA flag baked into an image config refused two evaluation runs before they began.

UPLOAD

A 4 GB zip wall, a killed SSH session and a refreshed token that turned 99.25% into zero. It took four tries.
